Sz34 Sonny: VR

(No interaction on the gaming rig) 1. Open Workstation: Sonny
2. to "set the stage" for this scene, you have to set it up:
   A) click the yellow program on the bottom
   B) select the scene entry #34 in the dropdown
3. switch to tab "Log"
4. control the 3d viewer:
How to control 3d window
Please a mouse as input device (not a trackpad)
  • Scrollwheel: zoom in & out
  • click & drag: rotate around center of landscape
  • cmd & click & drag: move camera left/right, or
  • right click & drag: move camera left/right

Actions in 3d window
  • Scrubbing:
  • Move the slider left & right to control the playback:
  •   -3 Really Fast backward
  •   -2 Fast backward
  •   -1 Play backward
  •   0 Pause
  •   1 Play
  •   2 Fast forward
  •   3 Really fast forward
  • Play/pause:
  •   Toggles between play and pause
  • Pause:
  •   Always pauses no matter the current state
  • SlowMo:
  •   Playback forawrd/backwards in slow motion
  • Reverse
  •   Play in reverse

5. click play, toggle the island, scrubb.
